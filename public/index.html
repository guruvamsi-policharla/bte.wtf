Batched threshold encryption

In batched threshold encryption (BTE), users encrypt to a committee of servers. Later, the committee can decrypt any chosen batch of ciphertexts with communication that is independent of, or sublinear in, the batch size. Ciphertexts outside the batch stay hidden as long as fewer than a threshold of servers collude.

The main application is encrypted mempools: transactions stay private until they are included in a block, which protects users from front-running and other forms of MEV.
